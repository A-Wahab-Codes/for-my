My dearest Maryam,

I know I hurt you. And there is no version of this that lets me off easy — I owe you more than that, more than empty words, more than a hurried "I'm sorry." You deserve to hear the real thing.

I thought about how to say this a hundred times, and every time I get to the part where I picture your face — that warmth you carry without even knowing it — the words just fall apart.

You are someone who deserves to be treasured, every single day. Not just when it's convenient. Not just when things are easy. I forgot that for a moment, and I hate that I did.

I know your heart. I know how much it takes for you to trust someone. And I know what it costs you when that trust gets bruised. I'm sorry, Maryam. Genuinely, completely sorry — not because I got caught, but because you felt the weight of something that I should never have let fall on you.

My heart still chooses you. In every quiet version of the future I imagine — it is still you. It has always been you.

I'm really sorry, Maryam. ❤️
My heart still chooses you. 🫶🏽
